Somma da 1 a 100

Consegna

Calcola la somma dei numeri da 1 a 100.

Somma dei numeri

Risultato:

-

Dettaglio:

-

Two methods: (1) A for loop accumulates a running total. (2) Gauss's closed-form formula: n*(n+1)/2 = 5050. Both produce the same result.

  • Gauss's formula: O(1) — no loop needed
  • The loop is O(n) — clear but inefficient at scale
  • Gauss discovered this at age 9 — mathematical insight beats brute force
