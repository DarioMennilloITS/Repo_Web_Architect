Numeri Pari

Consegna

Utilizzando if e for stampa solo i numeri pari da 1 a 100.

Numeri pari da 1 a 100

Output:

Clicca il pulsante per generare

Loops from 1 to 100 with a for loop. Each iteration checks n % 2 === 0 (even). Even numbers are collected in an array, then displayed joined with a separator.

  • n % 2 === 0 is the standard even-number test
  • Alternative: start at 2 and increment by 2 (i += 2) — no modulo needed
  • Collecting in an array then joining is cleaner than building a string in the loop
